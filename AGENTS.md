## Writing Guidelines

- Use a concise, first-person academic tone.
- Keep the README easy to scan: short introduction, research interests,
  selected highlights, and links.
- Prefer stable claims over constantly changing counts unless the count is
  verified from the homepage at edit time.
- Mention selected recent highlights only when they are present in the homepage
  source, such as ICML 2026, NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight, and ACL 2025 Findings.
- Avoid adding badges, decorative sections, or generated marketing copy unless
  explicitly requested.
